Neoclassical Canons & Golden Proportions

How Tool Genie translates classical aesthetics into objective, verifiable measurements.

The Law of Facial Thirds

The classical aesthetic standard divides the human face into three equal vertical heights: trichion to nasion (upper), nasion to subnasale (middle), and subnasale to gnathion (lower). Tool Genie computes percentage divergence from this exact 1:1:1 division.

In high-harmony faces, lower facial height is further divided at the stomion into an upper one-third (subnasale to upper lip) and lower two-thirds (lower lip to chin).

Horizontal Fifths & Canthal Vector

A harmonious face is precisely five eye-widths wide. The intercanthal distance between the eyes equals one eye-width, while the nose width aligns with the medial canthi.

Tool Genie measures the canthal axis inclination in degrees. A positive canthal tilt (+2° to +6°) provides alertness and youthful vitality, while negative tilt is quantified for aesthetic balance.
